Add in iCatcher

  1. Copy the feed address.
  2. Open the Podcasts tab and tap the button in the top-left corner.
  3. Tap Add Podcast Manually. If it doesn't pick up the copied address, paste it.
  4. Tap Continue, then Subscribe in the top-right.
